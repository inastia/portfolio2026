Corporate Gossip

Website Redesign | Client project | Sep 2026

Corporate Gossip Podcast had an outdated website with missing content, unclear navigation, and competing calls to action. I led the UX strategy, information architecture, UI design, and Squarespace development to create a website with accurate information, discoverable podcast content, and clear paths to subscribe and explore the podcast's growing business.

View Live Site →

Problem

👉 Transform an outdated website into a useful experience that supports subscriptions, content discovery, and the podcast's growing business.

The main navigation prioritized Contact, Advertise, and Sponsors, even though the podcast's primary goal was to gain subscribers. Visitors had no way to browse previous episodes, learn about the hosts, or easily access all four subscription platforms.

My Approach

👉 One primary goal - subscribe. Everything else gets its own place.
Decision 1: Make subscribing the primary action

I removed outdated sponsorship and advertising links from the main navigation and made Subscribe the most prominent action.

Instead of directing visitors to a single platform, I created a subscription pop-up offering four options: YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and Patreon.

Contact remained available but no longer competed with subscribing.

Decision 2: Make the podcast's content discoverable

The original homepage had little content beyond a featured appearance and an Apple Podcasts widget. I built out the homepage around the podcast’s own content:

  • Where to Listen: custom widgets for all four major platforms.
  • Previous Episodes: long-form episodes embedded from YouTube.
  • Shorts: short-form clips from YouTube.
Decision 3: Separate secondary business goals

I created dedicated About and Events pages with updated information about the podcast's team, upcoming appearances, and corporate ethics training. I also replaced direct email contact with a structured form for specific inquiries.

The Design

👉 Keep the personality. Improve the hierarchy.

I retained the podcast's existing visual identity and built a more consistent interface around it, with a defined typography scale, spacing system, reusable layouts, and orange accents to highlight key actions.

Beyond The Website

👉 One podcast, seven platforms.

I improved consistency across the podcast's broader digital presence. I reviewed Spotify, YouTube, Patreon, Apple Podcasts, TikTok, Instagram, and Bookshop.org, then standardized profile imagery, headers, About descriptions, and URLs where applicable.

Since launch, I’ve managed the podcast’s online presence as a whole, not just the website. I keep the Events page and the homepage’s Featured section current, including Becca’s latest guest appearance. I also monitor both hosts’ social profiles and Linktree pages, and send them specific update requests so every touchpoint stays consistent with the site.

Results

👉 A website that reflects the podcast today

The redesigned website launched on September 29, 2026.

The project replaced an outdated, sparse website with accurate information, clear navigation, and accessible podcast content. It also established dedicated paths for the podcast's growing business and a consistent identity across seven platforms.

Next Steps

The next phase focuses on making the website more useful as the podcast grows.

Custom audio player: Add an audio preview beneath the homepage hero so visitors can sample the show before subscribing.

Content expansion: Introduce Merch and transcript-based Blog content when the team has the resources to maintain them.
